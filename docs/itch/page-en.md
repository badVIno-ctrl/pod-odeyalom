# itch.io page — English

## Short description (≤ 140 characters)

A warm winter tale: a boy with a fever, a mom who stays close, and an old spider who plays fair to catch the fever moth.

## Description

**A boy has fallen ill.** His mom brought him through a blizzard to her old childhood home, and that night
the fever came. Mom takes care of him and hardly leaves his bed. But whenever she steps out for a minute,
an old house spider named **Tikhon** comes down from the corner.

Tikhon talks slowly, laughs "just for show", and knows who **Ember** is — a fire moth from the blizzard who
settles on those who burn. Catching her takes two. And it must be fair: *a thread spun out of pity won't hold.*

**Five days — five games with the spider:**

- tic-tac-toe in chalk;
- battleship with paper boats on the blanket;
- "Cobweb" (hangman) — every mistake adds a thread to the web;
- dots and boxes by the warm stove;
- checkers — the last, deciding match.

A fair win is a silver thread. Three threads make a net, and Ember is caught. A loss is her spark: the room
grows darker, and Mom calls the doctor. The score is shared, so it can all be decided by day three.

**Every morning you choose what to tell Tikhon.** Your answers unlock ten secrets — about Mom, the spider and
Ember — and lead to one of four endings. The diary remembers your endings, and Tikhon remembers you.

### Features

- 20–30 minutes per run, four endings, ten secrets, a hidden chapter and "Second Winter";
- three difficulty levels: the spider goes easy, plays for real, or forgives nothing;
- Mom gives a hint when your Courage meter is full;
- fully voiced in Russian (276 lines) with English subtitles;
- eight music themes grown from a single lullaby, and the song "Little Thread" in the credits;
- mouse, touch and keyboard; phones in landscape;
- works offline after the first load.

### Controls

Click, tap or **Space** — continue. **1**/**2** — answer Tikhon. Games — mouse or touch; letters in "Cobweb" —
from the keyboard. **H** — hint, **?** — rules of the current game.

### For parents

For kids 6+ and grown-ups. The story includes illness, fever and, in one ending, an ambulance — with nothing
frightening: grown-ups are always there, and doctors help. The spider is drawn kind. The game never suggests
treating yourself — Mom does the caring.

### About

All art is drawn in code, the music was written for the game. The voices are temporarily synthesized
(Silero TTS, non-commercial license), which is why the game is free. Credits and licenses are in the game.
